1. The app

Recharge lets you group apps into “batteries”. A battery drains while you use its apps and recharges while you are away. At 0% the apps are shielded using Apple’s Screen Time technology. Recharge is a self-management tool: you choose what to limit, and you can change or remove your limits.

These terms sit alongside Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the “Apple EULA”). If they conflict, the Apple EULA controls for anything it covers.

2. Screen Time permission

Recharge needs your permission to use Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) features. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings, after which Recharge can no longer shield your apps. iOS, not Recharge, decides what a shield can and cannot do.
