# Research: ai-agents-are-booking-contractors-now

Researched 2026-10-12 for the weekly Solvr Labs post. Primary sources preferred; vendor claims marked as such.

## Trend: AI agents now call and book local service businesses

1. **Google: agentic booking expands to local services; Google will call businesses for you in home repair, beauty, pet care; US rollout "this summer"; AI Mode passed 1B monthly users.**
   "A new era for AI Search", Elizabeth Reid, Google (The Keyword), May 19, 2026.
   https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/

2. **Google's agentic calling how-to page: you search "near me", Google asks a few questions, calls local businesses, sends you a summary by text/email. Live in the US.**
   "Ask Google to call local businesses for you", Google (The Keyword), published Nov 25, 2025, updated Jul 24, 2026.
   https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/how-to-agentic-calling-let-google-call/

3. **Google Business Profile help: Google may call/text the number on your profile when a customer wants to book, or to confirm price and availability of services; businesses can opt out in Business Profile settings > Advanced settings, or tell the caller to stop.**
   "About automated calls and texts from Google to your business", Google Business Profile Help (accessed Oct 12, 2026).
   https://support.google.com/business/answer/7690269?hl=en

4. **Earlier version: Google's AI started calling pet groomers etc. to check pricing ("Have AI check pricing"); Google said it only calls businesses that show up in normal local results for the query; opt-out in Business Profile.**
   "Google Search Can Now Call Local Businesses Using AI", Matt G. Southern, Search Engine Journal, Jul 16, 2025.
   https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-can-now-call-local-businesses-using-ai

5. **ServiceTitan launches Homh at Pantheon 2026: consumer platform connecting homeowners and their AI agents with selected ServiceTitan contractors; works with ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude; confirmed appointment booking into ServiceTitan. Also AdCap integration for AI CSR booking. No customer numbers disclosed.**
   "ServiceTitan Announcing New and Expanded Capabilities at Pantheon 2026", ServiceTitan press release, Oct 6, 2026.
   https://www.servicetitan.com/press/servicetitan-announcing-new-and-expanded-capabilities-at-pantheon-2026

6. **Coverage of Homh: listings show reviews, dispatch fees, completed-job counts; curated network in 11 metros; covers plumbing, heating and air, electrical; selected contractors only, no fee schedule disclosed; reporter didn't test booking through AI assistants.**
   "ServiceTitan Launches Homh for AI Search and Contractor Booking", Dispatch (TradeVulcan), Oct 2026.
   https://tradevulcan.com/dispatch/servicetitan-homh-ai-contractor-booking-chatgpt-2026

## Customer behavior

7. **45% of consumers used ChatGPT or other gen-AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6% the year before; 40% trust AI platforms for recommendations; Google's share of review usage fell from 83% to 71%; 74% only care about reviews from the last three months. Sample: 1,002 US adults.**
   "Local Consumer Review Survey 2026", Rosie Murphy, BrightLocal, Feb 11, 2026.
   https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/

8. **Home service owners: 52% use AI day to day (top uses quoting, invoicing, writing); over half of customers expect a reply within an hour, 28% expect immediate; 14% of customers value online booking; 23% of owners cite labor shortages as a growth barrier; 65% raised prices last year. Survey of 1,050 US owners, Dec 2025.**
   "Blue Collar Strong: The 2026 Home Service Trends Report", Jobber, 2026.
   https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/

## Case study (vendor-reported)

9. **Owned and Operated podcast #173 with Tyson Chen of Avoca: co-host John Wilson says his own company has used Avoca's AI answering for months, saving about $30,000/month, with call abandonment dropping from roughly 18-19% to about 7-8%. Chen: an unnamed Tennessee company went from a 43% booking rate with a national answering service to the low 90s in week one; full AI books about 5% lower than hybrid (AI + human), and he recommends hybrid for larger companies.** Vendor-reported, not independently verified.
   "Owned and Operated #173 - 'We Booked 400 Calls a Week': How Avoca AI is Shaping Home Services", Owned and Operated, Jan 23, 2026.
   https://www.ownedandoperated.com/post/owned-and-operated-173-we-booked-400-calls-a-week-how-avoca-ai-is-shaping-home-services
